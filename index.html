Apple Retail Analytics Dashboard

Demonstrates: Dashboard storytelling & analytical interpretation
VIEW DETAILED CASE STUDY
Problem Statement:

Apple retail stores lacked consolidated visibility into how trade-in promotions directly influenced accessory attachment rates and high-value product sales, leading to misaligned inventory.

Dataset & Context:

Simulated transactional database of 300,000+ retail records containing product lines, discount codes, customer type markers, and trade-in valuations.

Tools Used:
Tableau MS Excel Power Query
Analytical Approach:

Cleaned transaction logs using Power Query, verified database relationship integrity, created secondary classification metrics for trade-in cohorts, and designed dynamic dual-axis charts to trace seasonal purchase spikes.

Key Insights:

Transactions involving trade-in credits showed a 34% higher average order value (AOV) and a 1.8x higher accessory attachment rate (mostly protection plans and power accessories) compared to non-trade-in sales.

Business Impact:

Provided store managers with data-backed justification to bundle warranty services and cases directly inside trade-in workflows, driving a projected 12% revenue lift in accessory attachment sales.

Lessons Learned:

Handled high record counts directly in Tableau; learned that caching strategies and pre-aggregating data in Excel significantly improves dashboard load times and visual transition responsiveness.

COVID-19 Global Infection Analytics

Demonstrates: DAX modeling & interactive slicers
VIEW DETAILED CASE STUDY
Problem Statement:

Healthcare coordinators needed a unified reporting system to monitor regional case curves, testing ratios, and mortality lag times to optimize hospital bed distributions.

Dataset & Context:

Johns Hopkins epidemiological daily logs and WHO tracking reports covering global cases, recoveries, tests, and mortality tables.

Tools Used:
Power BI Power Query DAX Star Schema
Analytical Approach:

Key Insights:

Urban sectors showed a consistent 14-to-18-day lag time between case peaks and mortality surges. A 5% increase in regional testing coverage correlated with a 12% drop in hospital triage overrun.

Business Impact:

Constructed a predictive planning dashboard model helping simulated clinical networks preempt ICU capacity shortages 2 weeks in advance based on daily infection logs.

Lessons Learned:

Mastered DAX time-intelligence functions and calendar mapping; discovered that clear star-schema modeling is mandatory to prevent slow calculations on large datasets.

Hospital Patient Records Analysis

Demonstrates: Operational analysis & visual dashboard storytelling
VIEW DETAILED CASE STUDY
Problem Statement:

Hospital emergency rooms faced excessive patient wait times and triage backlogs, leading to drop-out rates and severe nursing staff exhaustion.

Dataset & Context:

10,000+ patient admission logs detailing timestamped check-ins, triage level indicators, treatment durations, and departmental assignments.

Tools Used:
Tableau MS SQL Server T-SQL Queries
Analytical Approach:

Wrote SQL queries to pre-aggregate admission logs, calculate differential wait intervals, model patient distributions by hour, and constructed interactive triage charts in Tableau.

Key Insights:

Triage Category 1 (critical cases) suffered a 45-minute average wait bottleneck between 5 PM and 8 PM daily, driven primarily by nurse shift overlaps and peak intake periods.

Business Impact:

Identified operational bottlenecks, showing that adjustments to nurses' shift-change schedules to cover the 5-8 PM peak would reduce urgent wait times by 30%.

Lessons Learned:

Learned how to process granular temporal differences across multi-system databases, and translate operational healthcare data into actionable, executive summaries.

Customer Churn Analysis

Demonstrates: Churn diagnostics & customer retention profiling
VIEW DETAILED CASE STUDY
Problem Statement:

A telecom provider experienced high subscriber attrition rates, but lacked clear segmentation data to identify pricing triggers and contract risk profiles.

Dataset & Context:

Telecom cohort database containing 7,000+ subscriber records outlining contract types, payment channels, monthly billings, and account cancellation flags.

Tools Used:
Tableau Python (Scikit-Learn) Excel Formulas
Analytical Approach:

Created subscriber tenure cohorts, built pricing sensitivity models using Excel pivots, and designed diagnostic retention matrices in Tableau to filter subscriber risk categories.

Key Insights:

Subscribers with Month-to-Month contracts paying via Electronic Check who used Fiber Optic lines showed a churn rate of 42.7% (vs a baseline of 26.5%), pointing to intense post-promo pricing spikes.

Business Impact:

Enabled retention teams to run automated campaign targets for high-risk cohorts (offering direct e-billing incentives), projecting a simulated 15% drop in subscriber loss.

Lessons Learned:

Learned the necessity of cohort segmentation; analyzing overall churn rate masks critical patterns found within specific subscription channels.
